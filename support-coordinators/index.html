Share the need. Keep the details.

What providers see:

the support types and a short description of the need, the suburb, when and how often, whether it's at home, and any preferences for the worker (for example language, age range, gender, religion or community).

What stays private:

names, contact details, diagnosis and NDIS plan - unless the person who posted chooses to share them.
